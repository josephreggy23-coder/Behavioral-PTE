# Acute Visual-Habituation Trajectories Classify a Later Seizure-Like Behavioral Label

Post-traumatic epilepsy can emerge after a clinically silent interval, yet scalable methods to identify early post-injury functional changes remain limited. Here, I developed a low-cost longitudinal assay that combines pressure-wave injury, infrared dark-flash tracking, and clutch-aware prediction in larval zebrafish. Wild-type AB larvae underwent sham, low-, or high-impact syringe pressure-wave exposure across three independent clutches. A custom 60-frames-per-second infrared rig recorded 30 dark-flash responses before injury and at 0.5, 1, 5, and 24 hours afterward. Responses were fit by nonlinear least squares to an exponential decay model, yielding the habituation time constant tau; larger tau denotes slower habituation. Across 133 larvae, 650 sessions yielded 19,500 trials. In 81 injured fish with complete pre-injury, 0.5-hour, and 24-hour trajectories, an L2-regularized logistic model combined baseline tau, within-fish changes at 0.5 and 24 hours, and injury dose to classify a supplied later seizure-like behavioral label. Nested leave-one-clutch-out validation achieved a mean-fold ROC AUC of 0.849, pooled out-of-fold AUC of 0.833, and 76.5% accuracy at a 0.50 threshold, with 66.7% sensitivity and 84.4% specificity. Within-clutch permutation testing that reran nested validation gave p = 0.001. At 0.5 hours, low- and high-impact injury produced opposing tau shifts (+3.74 versus −2.72 trials; d = 2.19, p < 0.0001), while a dose-blind behavioral model retained predictive performance (mean-fold AUC = 0.810), indicating predictive information beyond explicit dose encoding. In a separate molecular cohort, matched high-risk pools showed 27.5% higher *fosab* expression than low-risk pools (nominal paired p = 0.029), although this was not significant after clutch aggregation (p = 0.104). Sessions averaged 0.90 operator-minutes and $0.094 per fish. Dark-flash habituation kinetics offer a candidate behavioral assay for later seizure-like activity; the pipeline provides a reproducible framework for prospective electrographic validation.
